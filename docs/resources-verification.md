# Official Resources Verification Record

This document records the official verification of government portals and contact information included in OfferClara. All entries were verified against official Indian government portals on **20 October 2026**.

OfferClara makes zero outbound network calls to these services during runtime; these are static informational references provided to help students verify offers independently.

---

## 1. Verified Official Resources

### 1.1 National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal
- **Domain:** `cybercrime.gov.in`
- **Verified URL:** `https://cybercrime.gov.in`
- **Verified Helpline:** `1930` (National Cyber Financial Fraud Reporting Helpline, operated 24/7 by the Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre, Ministry of Home Affairs)
- **Verified Portal Claims:**
  - Online reporting portal for citizens to report cybercrime, specifically cyber financial fraud.
  - Guidance to retain transaction IDs, UTR numbers, chat messages, emails, and payment receipts as supporting evidence.
- **Verification Date:** 2026-10-20
- **Status:** All facts confirmed. None omitted.

---

### 1.2 AICTE National Internship Portal
- **Domain:** `aicte-india.org`
- **Verified URL:** `https://internship.aicte-india.org`
- **Verified Email:** `internship@aicte-india.org`
- **Verified Portal Claims:**
  - Registration and internship applications on the portal are 100% free of charge for students.
  - Employers and agencies are prohibited from charging fees (training fees, application fees, seat booking, or security deposits).
  - Demands for money related to applications should be reported directly to `internship@aicte-india.org`.
- **Verification Date:** 2026-10-20
- **Status:** All facts confirmed. None omitted.

---

### 1.3 National Career Service (NCS)
- **Domain:** `ncs.gov.in`
- **Verified URL:** `https://www.ncs.gov.in`
- **Verified Helpline:** `1514` (National toll-free career helpline under the Ministry of Labour and Employment)
- **Verified Email:** `support.ncs@gov.in`
- **Verified Portal Claims:**
  - Services provided by the National Career Service to job seekers are completely free.
  - Advisory warning job seekers never to pay employers, placement agencies, or interviewers for jobs or internships.
  - Verify NCS credentials and job postings exclusively via `ncs.gov.in`.
- **Verification Date:** 2026-10-20
- **Status:** All facts confirmed. None omitted.

---

### 1.4 Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) — Check Company / LLP Name
- **Domain:** `mca.gov.in`
- **Verified URL:** `https://www.mca.gov.in/content/mca/global/en/mca/fo-llp-services/company-llp-name-search.html`
- **Verified Portal Claims:**
  - Official public search service under MCA FO Services to look up registered companies and LLPs, their Corporate Identification Number (CIN) or LLP Identification Number (LLPIN), and registration status.
  - Search allows users to verify whether an entity exists under the exact legal name in Indian corporate registry records.
- **Contextual Disclaimer Added by OfferClara:**
  - A registered company does not prove that the sender or offer is genuine, and an unregistered name does not by itself prove that an offer is fraudulent (e.g. unregistered trading names, early-stage partnerships, or international entities).
- **Verification Date:** 2026-10-20
- **Status:** All facts confirmed. None omitted.
